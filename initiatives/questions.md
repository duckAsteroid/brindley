# Questions

From [GitHub #6](https://github.com/duckAsteroid/brindley/issues/6) and
[GitHub #7](https://github.com/duckAsteroid/brindley/issues/7), raised by an agent using Brindley on
a real project.

Open questions gate design: an initiative can't be `designed` while blocking ones remain. Two gaps
showed up in use. During implementation, an agent that hit a load-bearing decision didn't know a
blocking question would halt the work without reopening design, and nothing showed the initiative
as blocked. And in adopted files, decisions written as plain bullets under `## Open questions` were
counted as open, with only one-at-a-time tools to tidy them.
