# Adoption

From [GitHub #5](https://github.com/duckAsteroid/brindley/issues/5), reported while adopting a
collection of about 60 hand-written initiatives.

obvious repair — broken links after files moved, missing statuses, companion notes counted as
initiatives — and errors that don't say which folder they checked (as in
[GitHub #2](https://github.com/duckAsteroid/brindley/issues/2), fixed for `docs_impact`). The aim is
to make adoption one call plus a review rather than an afternoon of hand-editing.
